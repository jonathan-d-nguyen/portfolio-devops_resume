Selected engagements

The Walt Disney Company

Show systems for Galaxy's Edge and Runaway Railway

Implementation and troubleshooting on the multidisciplinary show networks behind two flagship attractions. Distributed systems, debugged under real pressure, in front of an audience that paid to be there.

United States Navy

A decade of secure IT networks

Ten years of IT where the standard for secure is not a checkbox with a friendly green tick next to it. Requirements gathering, testing and lifecycle management, on infrastructure that had to hold.

Built and run myself

How a project runs

01

A conversation about the business

Who buys from you, what they need to see, and what the site or the network has to do that it currently does not.

02

A fixed quote and a date

Written down and scoped in plain language, including what is not in scope. Half up front, half at launch.

03

The build, in the open

A link you can check any day of the week, and a short note every week on what moved. No disappearing for a month and coming back with a surprise.

04

Handover, properly

Accounts and domains in your name, a written record of how it is put together, and a walkthrough so you can make small changes without calling me. I will still answer if you call.
